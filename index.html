Forty years of a blessing.

For generations, Jewish mothers have hung the Shir Lamaalot, Psalm 121, beside the birthing bed and the cradle.

Forty years ago, when Bentzion Shemtov was born, the Lubavitcher Rebbe instructed his grandfather, Rabbi Avraham Shemtov, to place the Shir Lamaalot beside the baby in the hospital. [The family’s own telling goes here.]

“Do not wait for the mother and child to come home; start at the hospital!”The Lubavitcher Rebbe, Yud-Tes Kislev 5747 (1986)

Rochi Shemtov, the Rebbe’s emissary to Uruguay since 1985, now carries that blessing to every new mother.
